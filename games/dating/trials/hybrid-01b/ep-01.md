# Episode 3: Maximilian

Suitors, left to right: Sir Quill, Dash, Brock, Prince.
Cards: face up turn-on **Flattery**; hidden turn-on **Confidence**; hidden dealbreaker **Numbers and stats**.
Twist: **The Wingman** (target-right): Pitch the suitor on your right. Not yourself.
Question: Why you and not the three standing next to you?
Brain: model. Sealed from window.


## Walk-on (window A open, sealed)

**Maximilian** [intro]: Good evening. It is exhausting, being the best thing in every room I own.
**Sir Quill** [opener]: O thou, before whose beauty candles gutter, is this seat taken?
**Dash** [opener]: Hi! I prepared a joke. Cutting the joke. Keeping the hi. Hi!
**Brock** [opener]: Hi! Already counting your blinks. Don't stop. You're on eleven.
**Prince** [opener]: Dearest Beloved, URGENT: I have chosen you. Kindly confirm receipt.

## Round one (A locked)

**Maximilian** [question]: Why should you get the suite, and not the three beside you? I have seen their luggage.
**Sir Quill** [answer]: Why I, of all who stand before thee, plumed and polished, hopeful, glinting in the lamplight? Height.
**Maximilian** to Sir Quill: I would frame that. In a small, tasteful frame. *(charmed)* [hits: Confidence]
**Prince** [answer]: They are offering charm. I am offering a duchy. Kindly compare the brochures.
**Maximilian** to Prince: A fortune, you say. Reception will want a deposit first. *(unsure)*
**Brock** [answer]: Why me? Ask Denise. Eighty-one, deadlifts her grandson. Grandson's forty-four. He books it weekly.
**Maximilian** to Brock: Indoor voice, sir. Guests are sleeping in other shows. *(cringing)* [hits: Numbers and stats]
**Dash** [answer]: Three of them, one of me. I'm the rare one. Basic supply and demand.
**Maximilian** to Dash: Figures. Accounts is on the fourth floor. Take the stairs. *(cringing)* [hits: Confidence, Numbers and stats]
*Maximilian cuts Brock. The light goes out.*
**Maximilian**: Mr Brock, the kennel is round the back. It is a five-star kennel.
**Brock** [exit]: Cut! Fine! Pam got banned from three stores. Now it's five. MOMENTUM!

## Round two: the twist (B locked)

*The twist card turns: The Wingman, "Pitch the suitor on your right. Not yourself."*

**Maximilian** [twistintro]: Pitch the guest on your right. Sabotage is permitted if it is discreet.
**Sir Quill** (to Prince) [twist]: Prince is truly royal, fair one. I know, for I have paid his processing fee. It is processing.
**Maximilian** to Sir Quill: A fee is nothing. *(charmed)* [hits: Confidence] _(model)_
**Dash** (to Sir Quill) [twist]: Quill writes you a poem a day and never finishes one. So technically the love never ends. Exhausting.
**Maximilian** to Dash: Visor to the cloakroom, Mr Dash. Then we shall see. *(unsure)*
**Prince** (to Dash) [twist]: Congratulations, you have won one Dash. Lightly used. Pivots on contact. No returns.
**Maximilian** to Prince: Absurd. Generous. I am reaching for a pen. Stop me. *(charmed)*
**Dash** (to Sir Quill) [jab]: Lovely, Quill. Nobody understood it. Point to me.
**Sir Quill** (to Dash) [jab]: How darest thou speak prose in front of me?
*Maximilian cuts Dash. The light goes out.*
**Maximilian**: Mr Dash, you are blacklisted from every hotel I own. Which is most hotels.
**Dash** [exit]: Cut. Fine. I was going to leave anyway, so we wanted the same thing. Compatible.

## The booth (window C open)

*Standing: Sir Quill 6, Dash -2, Brock -4, Prince 1*

*Cut reason: standing -2; 0 charmed, 1 unsure, 1 cringing; 1x turn-on "Confidence"; 1x dealbreaker "Numbers and stats"; decided by Numbers and stats*

> **Sir Quill** (booth): A muse should sit still and be described. This one keepeth answering back. I have lost my place.
> **Prince** (booth, about Sir Quill): Sir Quill is charming. So was every footman my father ever dismissed.

## One on one (C locked)

**Prince** [duo]: Alone at last. Your briefing for Saturday's banquet: never sit beside the Duke. He proposes.
**Maximilian** to Prince: Your Highness, I have hosted eleven kings. None wore shorts. Defend the shorts. *(charmed)* [hits: Confidence]
**Prince** [duo]: Until tomorrow. Yours, Prince. Just Prince. The other forty titles can wait outside. _(fallback)_
**Sir Quill** [duo]: Now, with the knaves dismissed, the candles low, and nothing between us but this helm, hello.
**Maximilian** to Sir Quill: Sir Quill, the helm stays on. I have seen a knight's face before. *(cringing)* _(model)_
**Sir Quill** [duo]: Then go, and love a knave who rhymes with lunch.

## The pick

**Maximilian** [hold]: Silence. The last time I hesitated, a hotel burned down.
*Hold. Six seconds.*
**Maximilian** picks Prince: Your Highness, the suite. No fee. I have waived it. Tell nobody I waive things.
**Prince** [win]: Chosen. Splendid. P.S. Sir Quill starts in the stables tomorrow. Nobody has told them.

## The reveal

*A card turns.* **Maximilian**: Confidence. I have hidden it from three biographers. Well found.
  *Replay:* Prince: "Alone at last. Your briefing for Saturday's banquet: never sit beside the Duke. He proposes."
*A card turns.* **Maximilian**: Numbers and stats. I once had a bishop removed for that. He was mid-sentence.
  *Replay:* Brock: "Why me? Ask Denise. Eighty-one, deadlifts her grandson. Grandson's forty-four. He books it weekly."
**Sir Quill** [lose]: I lose to one who calleth Luna nice.

## Result (all windows settle)

**Maximilian** [outro]: That concludes the evening. I shall be up late, being right.
