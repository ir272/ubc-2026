## Round one (A locked)

**Vesper** [question]: I like expensive things and long silences. How do you afford either?
**Tilly** [answer]: Buy whatever you like, and I'll return it for you the moment you've finished being happy.
**Vesper** to Tilly: Stay on, Tilly. You're the first caller I didn't screen. *(charmed)*
**Brock** [answer]: Expensive taste? Eggs are expensive! I eat forty a day! We're RICH in eggs!
**Vesper** to Brock: You woke the trucker, Brock. He was doing so well. *(cringing)*
**Prince** [answer]: Easily. My cards are solid gold. Literally. No machine will take them, so tonight is on you.
**Vesper** to Prince: I had a fortune once. It's the tall mast outside. *(unsure)*
**Vex** [answer]: Your taste is expensive in exactly one area, and it's soap.
**Vesper** to Vex: Never surprised? I was surprised once. Nineteen sixty-six. Lovely. *(unsure)*
*Vesper cuts Brock. The light goes out.*
**Vesper**: Brock, you sound like dawn. I don't do dawn. Goodnight.
**Brock** [exit]: Three steps to the exit. I'll do it in two. Watch. Three. Doesn't count.

## Round two: the twist (B locked)

*The twist card turns: The Wingman, "Pitch the suitor on your right. Not yourself."*

**Vesper** [twistintro]: Sell me the caller on your right. Not you. If you sabotage them, be good at it.

**Vesper** to Tilly: Polite and lethal. I was like that in sixty-four. *(charmed)*
**Prince** (to Tilly) [twist]: Pick Tilly. She will make a superb lady-in-waiting. To you. At our wedding.
**Vesper** to Prince: That's how I sounded at launch. Still do. *(charmed)* [hits: Confidence]
**Vex** (to Prince) [twist]: Prince really is a prince. People who believed that also paid a small processing fee.
**Vesper** to Vex: Recommend me something, Vex. Something I haven't played. Go on. *(unsure)*
**Vex** (to Prince) [jab]: Prince, your crown shipped free with the shorts.
**Prince** (to Vex) [jab]: People like you also unsubscribed, Vex.

**Vesper**: For Vex, a song with no chorus. It just stops. Like this.
