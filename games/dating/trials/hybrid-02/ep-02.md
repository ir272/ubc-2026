**Vesper** to Brock: You woke the trucker, Brock. He was doing so well. *(cringing)*

## Round two: the twist (B locked)

*The twist card turns: The Wingman, "Pitch the suitor on your right. Not yourself."*

**Vesper** [twistintro]: Sell me the caller on your right, warmly, the way I sold war bonds, with the same honesty.

**Vesper** to Vex: Recommend something I haven't played, and I've played two wars. *(unsure)*
**Sir Quill** (to Vex) [twist]: Vex knoweth thy desires before thou dost. I asked her mine. She said goats.
**Vesper** to Sir Quill: I'm older than that, Quill, and I've been modernised twice. *(unsure)*
**Prince** (to Sir Quill) [twist]: Sir Quill is a true knight. I know, I knighted him. Small fee. He is still paying.

**Prince** (to Sir Quill) [jab]: Copying in the palace historian. That was a footnote.

*Vesper cuts Prince. The light goes out.*
**Vesper**: Prince, real kings have called this line, and not one asked for money.
**Prince** [exit]: Message recalled. Please disregard the kingdom. It was sent in error.
