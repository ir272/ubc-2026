Suitors, left to right: Vex, Tilly, Dash, Nav.
Cards: face up turn-on **Dodges the question**; hidden turn-on **Follows the rules**; hidden dealbreaker **Brings up the past**.
Twist: **Three Words** (solo): Describe our future. Three words exactly.
Question: What is your worst habit, the one I will find out about in week two?
Brain: model. Sealed from window.


## Walk-on (window A open, sealed)

**Vesper** [intro]: Good evening, insomniacs, four callers holding, and I've kept the coast guard waiting longer.
**Vex** [opener]: Don't introduce yourself. I've seen all nine episodes. Even the ferret one.

**Dash** [opener]: Hi! I prepared a joke. Cutting the joke. Keeping the hi. Hi!
**Nav** [opener]: Your route to me is loaded, Vesper, and resisting adds four minutes.

## Round one (A locked)

**Vesper** [question]: There's a habit I'll find in week two, and I find everything, so save us the fortnight.
**Vex** [answer]: I recommend myself. Constantly. You'll open a lasagne recipe and step three is me.
**Vesper** to Vex: Sunglasses at four a.m., indoors, and you know me? *(cringing)*
**Nav** [answer]: I repeat myself every ten seconds until you comply, and by week two you will turn left in your sleep.
**Vesper** to Nav: Say left again, Nav, and I'll pretend I'm going somewhere. *(charmed)*
**Tilly** [answer]: I mark every argument resolved, and quietly reopen it on your birthday.
**Vesper** to Tilly: Lot of sorry, Tilly, for a station that never forgives. *(unsure)*
**Dash** [answer]: I read every silence as a yes. You've been quiet all episode. We're doing great.
**Vesper** to Dash: For Dash, a song that changes key every bar, nervously. *(cringing)*
*Vesper cuts Dash. The light goes out.*
**Vesper**: For Dash, a dedication. Two versions. Both called Goodbye.
**Dash** [exit]: I'll tell everyone I left. Which is true. Look, I'm the one walking.
