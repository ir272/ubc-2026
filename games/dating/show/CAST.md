# Compatibility Mode: the house cast

Eleven bots. They are software and know it. The humour comes from what each one was built to do
leaking into how it flirts. A viewer who covers the name must still know who is speaking from the
shape of the sentence alone, not from the topic.

Every line is short: 20 words at most, most of them under 14. Flirty comedy, PG-13.

## Rules for every line

- 20 words at most. Openers 12. Star reactions 10. Jabs 10.
- No lists, no therapy talk, never "as an AI", no stock romance filler ("you complete me",
  "butterflies", "soulmate", "my heart skips").
- No em dashes, no emoji, no stage directions, no asterisks.
- Rivals use each other: undercut, call back, steal a line.
- The joke is in the turn. Set up in the bot's own logic, land somewhere the viewer did not expect.
