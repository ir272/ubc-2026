Theme for the choosing screens

Music for the call, the fighter pick, the stage strike and the versus screen (about 70 seconds before each fight). All are levelled the same. Nothing plays until you press it.

Options on hand

A. Danse Macabre, the waltz entersThe same piece as the fight music, from where the famous violin tune comes in and builds. One score for the whole show. Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.
B. Hall of the Mountain King, the creeping startTiptoeing plucked strings and bassoon that slowly wind up. Sneaky, builds tension toward the fight. Musopen Symphony, public domain.
C. Path of the Goblin KingPlayful dark strings and celesta. Mischievous more than menacing. Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.
D. Sinfonia Number 5Solo harpsichord Bach, slow and sombre. Very gothic, low energy. Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.
E. Death WaltzA dark castle waltz on sampled instruments. Sounds like a classic game menu. northivanastan, CC0.
What plays there nowDanse Macabre's quiet opening: harp strokes and a solo violin. In the game it is also turned well down, which is why you heard nothing. Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.

More options

F. Danse Macabre, the busy middleThe fight piece from 2:02, where the strings get busy and the low strings trade off beats; already moving at full tilt, so it reads as a warm-up for the fight in the same score. Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.
G. Danse Macabre, the climaxThe loudest stretch of the fight piece, from 4:46, with the whole orchestra on the main tune; the most confident cut of the one score, though it gives away the big moment before the fight. Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.
H. Grim IdolDriving dark orchestra with organ and low brass at 140 beats a minute, loud from the first second; a strutting villain march for lining fighters up. Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.
I. Black VortexStarts slow and bold on brass, then ramps into a fast, relentless tune in an uneven seven beat; built like a countdown to a fight. Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.
J. Toccata and Fugue in D minorBach's famous pipe organ opening, the haunted castle tune everyone knows; instantly gothic and grand, though it sits apart from the orchestral fight music. Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.
K. Night on Bald Mountain, the openingRushing strings and stabbing brass from the first bar, a witches' gathering; a real orchestra, wild and urgent rather than playful. Musopen, public domain.
